### Slide 3: Group Research
- Dissent is allowed

- Keep support for analysis and communications

- No front-running

## Whiteboard examples
1. **Right or wrong usage.** Students judge each: (a) "Ana Silva, CFA" - correct; (b) "Ana is a CFA" - incorrect, noun use; (c) "Ana passed all three levels on her first attempt" - correct if true; (d) "CFA Level II Ana Silva" - incorrect, partial designation; (e) "As a CFA charterholder, Ana will outperform the market" - incorrect, overstates; (f) "Ana is a Level II candidate in the CFA Program" - correct only while enrolled for the next exam.
2. **Trade ordering.** A client buy order, a firm proprietary buy order and the analyst's personal buy order all arrive for the same stock on the day a buy rating is published. Solution: execute client orders first, then the employer's, then the personal trade after clients have had an opportunity to act.
3. **Tobias case.** Holding: disclose prominently, trade after clients. Sister's fee-paying account: include in allocations normally. Referral fee: disclose nature and estimated value before clients engage. Signature: "Tobias Ekström, CFA."

## Questions to ask students
- Can you rely on research from a third party? (Yes, after reasonable checks of its soundness and independence.)
- How long should records be kept if no regulation applies? (At least seven years.)
- Should a family member's fee-paying account be excluded from a hot block trade? (No, treat it like any other client account.)
- When must referral fees be disclosed? (Before the client enters into the arrangement, including nature and estimated value.)
- Can a candidate tell friends which formulas appeared on her exam? (No, that is specific confidential exam content.)
- Is "John is a CFA" correct usage? (No, CFA must be used as an adjective.)

## Analogies
- A reasonable basis is a building's foundation: the taller the recommendation, the deeper the footings must be.
- Priority of transactions is a restaurant kitchen: customers are served before staff eat.
- Disclosure of conflicts is a food label: it does not make the product healthy, but it lets the buyer decide with full information.

## Recording tips
- This lesson is dense; use a one-page summary graphic of V, VI and VII sub-sections at start and end.
- Show the right-or-wrong designation list on screen and pause for students to answer.
- Emphasise that family fee-paying accounts are clients - students often get this backwards.

## Slides
### Slide 1: What the Income Statement Tells Us
- Revenues, expenses and profit over a period
- Accrual basis: earned and incurred, not cash
- Goal for analysts: sustainable, repeatable earnings
### Slide 2: Five-Step Revenue Model
- Identify the contract and the performance obligations
- Determine and allocate the transaction price
- Recognise revenue when control transfers (point in time or over time)
### Slide 3: Revenue Red Flags
- Cash received in advance is a liability, not revenue
- Bundles must be split by standalone selling prices
- Watch for revenue growing much faster than cash collections
### Slide 4: Expense Recognition
- Matching: COGS recognised with the related sale
- Period costs expensed as incurred
- Estimates (bad debts, warranties, useful lives) shift profit between years
### Slide 5: Non-Recurring Items
- Discontinued operations: separate line, net of tax, below continuing operations
- Unusual or infrequent items: inside continuing operations, pre-tax
- Policy changes retrospective; estimate changes prospective
### Slide 6: Basic EPS
- (Net income - preferred dividends) / weighted average shares
- Weight issuances and buybacks by months outstanding
- Splits and stock dividends applied retroactively
### Slide 7: If-Converted Method
- Convertible preferred: add back preferred dividends, add conversion shares
- Convertible debt: add back interest × (1 - t), add conversion shares
- Test: add-back per new share vs basic EPS
### Slide 8: Treasury Stock Method
- Assume options exercised; proceeds buy back shares at average price
- Incremental shares = N - N×K / average price
- Only dilutive when average price exceeds exercise price
### Slide 9: Antidilution
- If including a security raises EPS, exclude it
- Diluted EPS is never above basic EPS
- Rank securities from most to least dilutive when several exist
### Slide 10: Halden Corp Answer
- Weighted shares 5,900,000; basic EPS 1.86
- 800,000 conversion shares + 80,000 option shares
- Diluted EPS 1.77
### Slide 11: Key Takeaways
- Control transfer drives revenue timing
- Separate one-offs to find sustainable profit
- Know both dilution methods cold

## Whiteboard examples
**Example 1 - Halden Corp.** NI 12,000,000; preferred dividends 1,000,000; 5,000,000 shares at 1 Jan; 1,200,000 issued 1 Apr; preferred convertible into 800,000 shares; 400,000 options at strike 20, average price 25.
- Weighted shares = 5,000,000 + 1,200,000 × 9/12 = 5,900,000.
- Basic EPS = 11,000,000 / 5,900,000 = **1.86**.
- Preferred add-back per share = 1.25 < 1.86, dilutive.
- Options: 400,000 - 8,000,000 / 25 = 80,000 incremental shares.
- Diluted EPS = 12,000,000 / 6,780,000 = **1.77**.

**Example 2 - Weighted shares with a buyback.** 2,000,000 shares at 1 Jan, 600,000 issued 1 Jul, 200,000 repurchased 1 Oct. Weighted = 2,000,000 + 600,000 × 6/12 - 200,000 × 3/12 = **2,250,000**. With NI 5,000,000 and preferred dividends 500,000, basic EPS = 4,500,000 / 2,250,000 = **2.00**.

**Example 3 - Antidilutive options.** 100,000 options at strike 30, average price 25: the options are out of the money, so no incremental shares are added. Explain that exercising them would be irrational.

## Questions to ask students
- A customer prepays a 12-month subscription. How much revenue is recognised on day one? (None; it is a contract liability recognised over the 12 months.)
- Are discontinued operations reported before or after tax? (Net of tax, below continuing operations.)
- Why subtract preferred dividends in basic EPS? (They are not available to common shareholders.)
- When are options dilutive? (When the average market price exceeds the exercise price.)
- Can diluted EPS be higher than basic EPS? (No; antidilutive securities are excluded.)

## Analogies
- Revenue recognition is like a gym membership: the gym earns your annual fee month by month as it delivers access, not when you swipe your card.
- The treasury stock method is a "net issuance": the company hands out new shares but uses the cash it receives to buy some back, so only the leftover shares dilute.
- Discontinued operations are a sold-off wing of a house: you still report what it cost you this year, but you do not include it when estimating next year's heating bill.

## Recording tips
- Build the diluted EPS calculation column by column on screen (numerator adjustments, denominator adjustments) so students see the structure.
- Students lose marks on month counting; draw a 12-month timeline for each share event.
- Emphasise the quick dilution test because it saves time on exam day.
